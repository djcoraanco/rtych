The familiarity of a book. The possibilities of connected learning.

READTYCH brings reading, handwriting, and classroom learning together through a two-screen Digital Book and a connected educational platform. Designed around students and educators, it creates a more organized, focused way to learn, teach, and grow.

Product

Two Screens. One Connected Learning Experience.

Read a lesson on one screen. Take notes, solve a problem, or complete an assignment on the other.

READTYCH's book-style design keeps learning materials and the student's workspace side by side. Paper-like displays and natural stylus handwriting preserve the familiar experience of reading and writing while connecting students to their classroom resources.

Read With Focus

Keep textbooks, lessons, and reference materials together in a device purpose-built for education.

Write Naturally

Take handwritten notes, work through ideas, and complete exercises with a stylus. READTYCH supports the way students develop their thinking on the page.

Keep Learning Organized

Bring class materials, notes, assignments, and feedback into one coordinated environment, making it easier to find what is needed and pick up where learning left off.

Ecosystem

More Than a Device. A Connected Educational Ecosystem.

READTYCH connects the student's Digital Book with a centralized platform for educators, schools, and education systems.

For Students

A clearer path through the school day. Access learning materials, organize notes, complete assignments, and review feedback within a focused learning environment.

For Educators

Tools that support teaching and meaningful feedback. Distribute resources, organize classroom activities, review assignments, and follow student progress to understand where additional support may be needed.

For Schools and Education Systems

A coordinated approach to managing learning. Organize classrooms, users, and resources while maintaining control over approved content, access, and academic policies.

Technology

Technology Designed to Support Better Learning

Meaningful learning takes attention, practice, and support. READTYCH is being developed to make those essentials easier to bring together.

01

More room to think

Keeping reading materials and a writing space visible together lets students work through ideas without repeatedly switching between views.

02

A simpler school day

Connected materials, assignments, and feedback help students and educators spend less time navigating disconnected tools.

03

Earlier opportunities to help

Progress visibility can help educators recognize where students need additional guidance and make more informed decisions about their support.

04

Less reliance on printed materials

Digital textbooks, notes, and assignments can reduce the need to print and distribute classroom resources.

Intelligent Support. Educators in Control.

READTYCH is being developed with intelligent features that support teachers and help make learning information more useful. These include handwriting recognition, assistance with assignment review and grading workflows, and progress summaries that help educators identify potential learning gaps.

Teachers and authorized school personnel remain responsible for grades, feedback, and academic decisions. Schools retain control over the curriculum and learning resources provided to students.

About READTYCH

We Believe Everyone Is a Genius.

READTYCH is an education technology company founded in Houston, Texas, building a connected educational ecosystem around a patented two-screen Digital Book.

Our mission is to make illiteracy a problem of yesterday by helping students access the tools, resources, and support they need to learn, grow, and share.

We believe educational technology should fit the way people learn and help educators bring out each student's potential.
